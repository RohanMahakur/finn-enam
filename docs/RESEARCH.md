# Rules first, LLMs last: building FINN

FINN can be built almost entirely on free data and cheap models, as long as it treats exchange filings as structured data and keeps the LLM away from routine work. For data, NSE's corporate-announcements and results JSON APIs work but sit behind Akamai bot detection, so they need `curl_cffi` TLS impersonation and warmed-up cookies. BSE's announcements API works with plain `requests` and is the natural fallback. screener.in fills in parsed financials and links to concall transcripts and annual reports. On regulation, SEBI LODR does much of the product design for us. A **"brief profile" is a mandatory field when a director or KMP is appointed**, so the filing is the primary source for management-change bios. Board decisions, results included, must be filed **within 30 minutes** of the meeting closing, and **concall transcripts reach the exchanges within 5 working days**. The pull agent should therefore start from the transcript filing, not the results filing. On models, a three-tier router (regex rules → Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini Flash-Lite or Groq gpt-oss-20b → a Flash or Sonnet-class model for concall analysis) brings the cost per filing down to fractions of a cent. On product, watchlist alerts, chat over filings and live transcripts are already commodity. The open ground is **materiality ranking, "unexplained move" alerts and a guidance-vs-delivery credibility ledger**. Nothing here was live-tested against the exchanges: the research sandbox could not reach nseindia.com or api.bseindia.com, and all endpoint details come from reading the source of maintained open-source libraries. Several model names are newer than the researcher's training data and must be confirmed in each provider's console.

## NSE needs a browser fingerprint; BSE and screener cover the gaps

The NSE announcements endpoint is `GET {BASE}/api/corporate-announcements?index=equities&symbol=X&from_date=..&to_date=..`. It returns a plain JSON list with `symbol, desc, an_dt, sort_date, attchmntFile, attchmntText, sm_isin, seq_id, exchdisstime`, and the PDFs are hosted on `nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/` ([NseIndiaApi sample](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BennyThadikaran/NseIndiaApi/main/src/samples/announcements.json)). nsepython's maintainers explain that NSE is fronted by **Akamai Bot Manager, which fingerprints the TLS handshake**. A plain `requests.Session()` gets a 403 even on the homepage, and this is bot detection, not geo-blocking. Their fix is `curl_cffi` with `impersonate="chrome124"`, warm-up GETs to the homepage and `/option-chain`, and a `Referer` set to an nseindia page ([nsepython rahu.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aeron7/nsepython/master/nsepython/rahu.py)). The sources disagree on the domain. NseIndiaApi v5.1.3 says NSE moved to `www.nseindia.in` and keeps archives on `.com` ([NseIndiaApi README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BennyThadikaran/NseIndiaApi/main/README.md)), while nsepython still calls `www.nseindia.com`. **Make BASE a config value and try both.** The date format (`DD-MM-YYYY`) and whether NSE blocks datacenter IPs are both **unverified**.

For headline numbers, `GET {BASE}/api/results-comparision?symbol=X` (NSE's own misspelling) returns about five quarters of parsed P&L under `resCmpData`. The keys include `re_net_sale`, `re_pro_loss_bef_tax`, `re_net_profit`, `re_int_new`, `re_depr_und_exp` and `re_basic_eps_for_cont_dic_opr`, plus bank-specific fields such as `re_int_earned`, `re_int_expd` and `re_grs_npa_per`. **All values are strings, in Rs lakhs** ([NseIndiaApi sample](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BennyThadikaran/NseIndiaApi/main/src/samples/results_comparison.json)). `corporates-financial-results` returns filing metadata with a per-filing `xbrl` URL, with standalone and consolidated as separate rows, but no revenue or EPS ([NseIndiaApi NSE.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BennyThadikaran/NseIndiaApi/main/src/nse/NSE.py)). Whether `results-comparision` returns standalone or consolidated figures is **unverified**; the RELIANCE sample looks standalone. The XBRL concept names (`RevenueFromOperations`, etc.) are **inferred, not read**, so if XBRL is used, parse it by local-name.

BseIndiaApi uses plain `requests` with browser `Origin`/`Referer` headers and warns that a typical day can have "50+ pages and 2K+ announcements", so filter by scrip code ([BseIndiaApi BSE.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BennyThadikaran/BseIndiaApi/main/src/bse/BSE.py)). Map NSE symbols to BSE codes **by ISIN**: NSE carries `sm_isin`, and BSE `lookup()` returns `isin` and `bse_code`. Cache the mapping once as a CSV. The screener.in public page needs no login. Its quarterly rows are `Sales +, Expenses +, Operating Profit, OPM %, Other Income +, Interest, Depreciation, Profit before tax, Tax %, Net Profit +, EPS in Rs`. Its Documents block lists annual reports (pointing at BSE `AttachHis`) and concalls with `Transcript / AI Summary / PPT / REC` links ([screener TCS](https://screener.in/company/TCS/consolidated)). The HTML ids (`section#quarters`, `table.data-table`, `ul#top-ratios`) and the bank row labels are **from memory, unverified**, so key the parser on labels and section headings, not positions. yfinance had persistent rate-limiting from April 2025 that upgrades fixed for some users but not all ([yfinance #2422](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2422)), which makes the daily bhavcopy the safer price source. The bhavcopy is one request per day for the whole market.

## SEBI LODR fixes the push schedule and supplies the bios

The disclosure rules map straight onto FINN's triggers. Reg 30(6) requires disclosure within **30 minutes of the board meeting closing** for board decisions, 12 hours for events originating inside the company, and 24 hours for external events. Financial results from multi-day meetings fall under a 30-minute or 3-hour window ([ca2013.com Reg 30](https://ca2013.com/lodr-regulation-30/)). For analyst and investor calls, Schedule III para A.15 requires **two working days' notice**, presentations before the call starts, **audio on the company website within 24 hours** (or before the next trading day if sooner), video within 48 hours, and **transcripts on the website and to the exchanges within five working days** ([ca2013.com Schedule III](https://ca2013.com/schedule/lodr-schedule-iii-part-2/)). Audio goes only on the company website, while transcripts reach NSE/BSE as a separate announcement. So the pull pipeline's "new concall" trigger is the transcript filing, and audio would need company-site scraping.

For management changes, the July 2023 circular (SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123) requires the reason for the change, the date and term, a **"brief profile (in case of appointment)"**, and relationships between directors ([LegalMantra](https://legalmantra.net/blog-detail/What-are-the-disclosures-applicable-for-Change-in-Directors-Key-Managerial-Personnel-of-Director-in-a-Listed-Entity); [Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-specifies-detailed-disclosures-to-be-made-under-regulations-30-30a-of-sebi-lodr-regulations)). FINN should extract the bio from the filing first and use web search only when the profile is thin. The "relationships" field gives a free promoter-family flag. Resignations carry stronger signals. Detailed reasons for an **auditor resignation** are due within 24 hours of the company receiving them. Independent director, KMP and senior-management resignations require the resignation letter and reasons within seven days. If the MD or CEO is unable to perform the role for over 45 days in any rolling 90, that must be disclosed too ([ca2013.com Schedule III](https://ca2013.com/schedule/lodr-schedule-iii-part-2/)). The circular PDF itself was not fetched; the field list rests on secondary sources that agree with each other.

BSE-style subjects follow a `Category-Subcategory` pattern, such as "Announcement under Regulation 30 (LODR)-Change in Directorate", "-Resignation of Statutory Auditors", "-Analyst / Investor Meet - Outcome", "Board Meeting Outcome for …", "Disclosure As Per Regulation 7(2) Of SEBI (PIT)…" and "Disclosure Under Regulation 29(2) Of SEBI (SAST)…" ([research360 listing](https://research360.in/stocks/upl-ltd/newsannouncements)). A prefix regex therefore covers most traffic. NSE's shorter `desc` values ("Financial Result Updates", "Change in Directors/ Key Managerial Personnel/ Auditor/…", "Analysts/Institutional Investor Meet/Con. Call Updates", the catch-all "Updates") are **background knowledge, unverified**. The exact subject string for SAST Reg 31 pledge filings is also unverified.

The quarterly results line items and the EBITDA convention were **not sourced this session**. The working assumption is the Ind AS line items from Revenue from operations through EPS. Operating EBITDA is taken as PBT before exceptional items + finance costs + D&A − other income; store the variant that includes other income as well and label which one is shown. Banks are different: report NII (interest earned − interest expended), PPOP, provisions, PAT and GNPA/NNPA, never EBITDA. Units vary between lakh, crore and million, so parse the units header. Verify all of this against the SEBI format circular before writing final regexes.

Do not let a demo depend on the Gemini free tier alone; Groq's published free limits are the more predictable free fallback. Exact Claude API ID strings (likely `claude-haiku-5-5`) and Gemini's OpenAI-compatible base URL are **unverified**.

The routing works like this. Tier 0 handles the bulk of filings with free regex rules on `desc` and subject text. Tier 1 classifies the ambiguous "Updates" bucket and extracts JSON fields (bio, results numbers, guidance claims). Tier 2 runs concall and annual-report analysis, keeping each chunk **under 100k tokens to avoid Haiku 5.5's long-context surcharge**. A Gemini Flash vision call is the fallback for scanned PDFs: flag a page as scanned when it yields fewer than about 50–100 characters of text, a threshold that is **unsourced**. Wrap all providers in LiteLLM, which exposes `completion_cost()` and `response._hidden_params["response_cost"]`. Call `register_model()` for any model missing from its community cost map ([LiteLLM docs](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/completion/token_usage.md)), which will probably apply to the 5.5 and 3.8-series models. A SQLite cache keyed on `(filing_id, prompt_version)` makes reruns free and deterministic.

When the filing's own profile is thin, enrich management-change bios with search-API snippets and never fetch LinkedIn pages, since LinkedIn's terms prohibit scraping. Free quotas as of late October 2026 were **Tavily 1,000 credits/month (no card), Serper 2,500 one-time queries, Brave about 1,000/month (card and attribution required), and Exa $10/month credit** ([Parallel.ai comparison](https://parallel.ai/ai/articles/best-free-web-search-api)). These figures come from a single comparison article, not from vendor pages. Tavily's `include_domains` parameter and the `site:linkedin.com/in` operator are **unverified** from vendor docs.

## Ranking and say-do tracking are the open ground

Indian incumbents already cover the basics. Screener turns a watchlist into "a personal news feed" with email result updates and disclosure alerts ([Screener features](https://screener.in/features)), but it has no native app, and its free tier is limited to 10 stock alerts with "basic, delayed" email ([Strike review](https://www.strike.money/reviews/screener-in); note that Strike is a competitor). Trendlyne asks users to configure six alert types ([Trendlyne help](https://help.trendlyne.com/support/solutions/articles/84000396716-what-are-the-different-types-of-alerts-)). Perplexity has offered **free live transcripts of Indian earnings calls since August 2025** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/18/perplexity-now-supports-live-earnings-call-transcripts-for-indian-stocks/)). Quartr's mobile app traces "every output … to its original source" ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/mobile-app)). Hudson Labs extracts hard and soft guidance with quotes, but compares it **to prior guidance, not to reported actuals** ([Hudson Labs](https://hudson-labs.com/post/management-guidance-summary-in-earnings-call)). FINN should not compete on feeds, threshold alerts, transcripts or cited chat. It should compete on **judgement**: ranking alerts by materiality and tracking whether management delivers on its guidance. Tijori, Koyfin, AlphaSense and similar products were not checked.

For push design, Google SRE's rule maps well. Interrupt a person only for real, ongoing, actionable problems, using "simple to understand" rules, and avoid "'magic' systems that try to learn thresholds" ([Google SRE ch. 6](https://www.oreilly.com/radar/monitoring-distributed-systems)). The consumer data points the same way: **irrelevance (30%) ranks above volume (25%)** as a reason for push opt-out, though the survey is small and dated, from 2016 with n=14 ([Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/opt-out-of-push-notifications-why-users-do-it)). Learned ranking in Gmail Priority Inbox saved users about **6% of email-reading time** ([Softpedia](https://news.softpedia.com/news/Gmail-s-Priority-Inbox-Explained-in-Google-Research-Paper-176954.shtml)). The resulting stance is **transparent rule gates for push, learned ranking only inside the digest, and a one-line "why this fired" on every alert**. Tiers: *push* covers results surprises, guidance cuts, auditor or KMP resignations, pledge spikes and unexplained moves. *Digest* covers everything else, in a "since you last looked" brief. *Log only* covers trading-window closures and newspaper ads. A single noise-floor slider mapped to the materiality threshold replaces Trendlyne's alert matrix. These UI ideas are design choices and have not been validated.

Threshold alerts should use standard event-study measures. Abnormal log-volume is `log(V_t+1)` minus its mean over the estimation window. Abnormal volatility is `R²_t/σ̂² − 1`. A typical estimation window is 150 days ending 11 days before the event ([EventStudy vignette](https://rdrr.io/cran/EventStudy/f/vignettes/volume-volatility-event-study.Rmd); [EventStudyTools](https://eventstudytools.com/volume-event-study)). Compute the abnormal return as the residual from a market model against Nifty or the sector index, then turn it into a z-score, using median/MAD for robustness. Retail threshold values such as |z|>2 or 3, or 2× the 20-day volume average, are **unsourced**. When a move crosses the thresholds, link it to any filing or news in the window. A large move with high volume and no disclosure gets its own push class. An explained move goes into the digest with its cause attached.

Pull research gets its signature feature from the academic literature. Ng, Tuna & Verdi measure credibility as the average scaled accuracy of a firm's past forecasts. For firms with more accurate track records, the market's immediate reaction to a forecast surprise is **3.05% higher**, and 3-month post-forecast drift falls **from 4.60% to 1.59%** from the least to the most credible firms, with the effect significant for quarterly forecasts ([Ng, Tuna & Verdi](https://core.ac.uk/download/13249539.pdf)). Managers also bias forecasts strategically: distressed firms tend to be optimistic ([Knowledge at Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/how-credible-are-management-forecasts-not-very/)). Extract each concall guidance claim as a record of (metric, period, value or range, hard/soft, verbatim quote, speaker). When that period's results arrive, resolve each claim as delivered, missed or **silently dropped**. FutureSearch reports that dropped metrics and new hedges ("excluding", "subject to") signalled reduced confidence in 27 of 50 releases its author reviewed ([FutureSearch](https://futuresearch.ai/ai-earnings-analysis)). Show the result as a ledger with a track-record badge on new guidance. Every pull answer should cite page and paragraph in the annual report or transcript. Quartr has made source citation table stakes, so the finer granularity is where FINN can differ. The precision of LLM guidance extraction was not measured anywhere in the research.

## Conclusion

The regulation does more of FINN's work than any model. The mandated 30-minute results window, the 5-working-day transcript deadline, the mandatory brief profile and the `Category-Subcategory` subject strings mean that timing, triggers, bios and most classification can be handled deterministically. The LLM budget can then go almost entirely to the two tasks rules cannot do: extracting guidance claims and judging materiality. That fits a free-tier constraint well, and it is also a defensible architecture to present to a reviewer, because every push decision can be explained.

The main engineering risk is data access, not model choice. NSE's bot detection and the unresolved domain question could break the push feed from a cloud host. So build the BSE fallback and the ISIN mapping on day one, cache everything, and treat screener.in as a convenience rather than a dependency. The main product bet is the credibility ledger. None of the products surveyed tracks guidance against delivery across quarters, and published research shows that the market itself prices that track record.
